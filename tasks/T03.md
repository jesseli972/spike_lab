# T03: Ball

Read `CLAUDE.md` before starting.

**Goal:** a ball whose spin is visible.

**Files:** `src/scene/ball.js`. **Needs:** T01.

1. Make a sphere of radius `BALL_RADIUS` with 32 segments.
2. Draw its texture on a canvas: an original design of three colored bands or panels, clearly not uniform, so rotation shows.
3. Use `MeshStandardMaterial` with roughness about 0.6. The mesh casts a shadow.
4. `setState(pos, quaternion)` copies both onto the mesh. `setVisible(bool)` shows or hides it.

**Done when:**

- A temporary test that rotates the ball each frame shows the pattern turning.
- The mesh's bounding sphere radius equals `BALL_RADIUS`.
- The temporary test code is removed before you report.

**Watch out:** do not copy a real ball brand's design or logo.
