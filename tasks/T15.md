# T15 (stretch): Slow-motion replay

Read `CLAUDE.md` before starting.

**Goal:** replay a finished flight slowly and scrub through it.

**Files:** `src/main.js`, `src/ui/panel.js`. **Needs:** T12. **Who:** you or your strongest agent, because it extends the panel contract.

1. Add to the panel: a playback speed slider (0.1 to 1), a pause toggle, and a scrub slider from 0 to the flight time.
2. Add matching callbacks to `createPanel`. This changes a contract, so the owner (not an agent) updates the module contracts in `CLAUDE.md` to match.
3. In `main.js`, speed sets `timeScale`, and scrubbing sets `time` directly and redraws the ball and trail.

**Done when:** a finished flight can be replayed at quarter speed and scrubbed backward and forward.
