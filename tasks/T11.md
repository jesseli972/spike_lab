# T11: Integration and playback

Read `CLAUDE.md` before starting.

**Goal:** wire every module together so pressing Launch plays a full flight.

**Files:** `src/main.js`. **Needs:** T02 to T10. **Who:** you or your strongest agent.

1. Build the scene from the real modules: court, ball, lights, one trail, marker, camera rig, panel, readout. Remove the temporary grid.
2. Keep one playback object: `{ result, time, playing, timeScale: 1 }`.
3. On launch: run `simulate(launch)`; call `trail.setPath` and `trail.reveal(0)`; hide the marker; clear the readout; call `rig.setServerPos(launch.pos)`; reset the ball's rotation; set `time = 0` and `playing = true`.
4. Each frame while playing: add `dt * timeScale` to `time`. Find the two samples around `time` and interpolate the position. Rotate the ball's quaternion about the `omega` axis by `|omega| * dt * timeScale`. Call `ball.setState`, `trail.reveal(time)`, and `rig.update(dt, pos, vel)`.
5. When `time` reaches `flightTime`: snap to the last sample, stop playing, show the marker, and call `readout.show(result)`.
6. While not playing, call `rig.update(dt, null, null)`.
7. On net-height change: remove and dispose the old court group and create a new one. On view change: call `rig.setView`.

**Done when:**

- Pressing Launch with default settings flies the ball, grows the trail, and ends with a marker.
- The flight lasts `result.flightTime` seconds of real time, within 0.1 s.
- Launching again mid-flight restarts cleanly with no leftover trail.
- All five views work during a flight.
- After switching net height, the net moves and the next launch uses the new height.
- The browser console shows no errors or warnings.
