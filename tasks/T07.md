# T07: Trail and landing marker

Read `CLAUDE.md` before starting.

**Goal:** a thick line along the ball's path, and a ring where it lands.

**Files:** `src/scene/trail.js`, `src/scene/marker.js`. **Needs:** T01.

1. Trail: use `Line2`, `LineGeometry`, and `LineMaterial` from `three/addons/lines/`, with a line width of 4 pixels and the given color.
2. `setPath(samples)`: dispose the old geometry, build a new one from every sample position, and keep the sample count.
3. `reveal(t)`: show the path only up to time `t` by setting the geometry's `instanceCount` to `floor(t / PHYSICS_DT)`, clamped between 0 and the sample count minus 1.
4. `clear()`: hide the trail.
5. Marker: a flat ring (inner radius 0.12, outer 0.2) lying on the floor at y = 0.003.
6. `show(landing, outcome)`: move the ring to the landing point; green for `'in'`, red for anything else. If `landing` is `null`, hide it. `hide()` hides it.

**Done when:**

- A temporary fake arc of 200 samples appears bit by bit as `reveal` is called with a growing `t`.
- The line is clearly thicker than 1 pixel.
- The marker shows green and red at a point you choose.
- The temporary code is removed before you report.

**Watch out:** if `Line2` will not work, fall back to `THREE.Line` with `setDrawRange` and list it under DEVIATIONS.
