# T02: Court

Read `CLAUDE.md` before starting.

**Goal:** a regulation court, net, posts, and antennas, to scale.

**Files:** `src/scene/court.js`. **Needs:** T01.

1. Floor: a 30 × 20 plane at y = 0, in a neutral color. It receives shadows.
2. Court surface: an 18 × 9 plane at y = 0.001, in a different color. It receives shadows.
3. Lines: white, `LINE_WIDTH` wide, at y = 0.002. Two sidelines and two end lines with their outer edges exactly on the court bounds. One center line at x = 0. Two attack lines whose far edges are `ATTACK_LINE_DIST` from x = 0.
4. Net: a plane in x = 0, `NET_LENGTH` wide in z, `NET_BAND` tall, with its top edge at `netHeight`. Use a canvas-drawn grid texture with transparency, and a white band along the top edge.
5. Posts: two cylinders at z = ±(4.5 + `POST_OFFSET`), `POST_HEIGHT` tall, radius 0.05.
6. Antennas: two thin cylinders (radius 0.01) at z = ±4.5, from the net's bottom edge to `ANTENNA_ABOVE_NET` above its top, with red and white stripes.
7. Net, posts, and antennas cast shadows. Give every mesh a `name`.

**Done when:**

- A `THREE.Box3` around the court surface measures 18 × 9.
- The net's top edge is at 2.43 for `createCourt(2.43)` and at 2.24 for `createCourt(2.24)`.
- Lines do not flicker when you orbit the camera.
- You can see through the net.

**Watch out:** planes face +z by default; rotate floor planes by −90° about X.
